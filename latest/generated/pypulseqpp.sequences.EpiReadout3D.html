EpiReadout3D#

class pypulseqpp.sequences.EpiReadout3D[source]#

Bases: SequenceModule

A slab-selective EPI train, phase-encoded along y and partition-encoded along z.

fov and matrix take three values, readout first. The partition axis is blipped line by line, so a whole CAIPI pattern is played inside one train.

The caller sets the shot origin by scaling the prewinders. Blips step between the lines of order and share the readout ramps; with flyback they play in the rewind gaps instead. TE is timed to the line te_line (the first by default), because which line samples the centre of k-space depends on the shot origin the caller sets.

Navigator lines are read after the read prewinder and before the phase-encode prewinder, without blips, so they sample the centre line at alternating polarities; the train continues the alternation.

Parameters:

  • system (pypulseqpp.Opts) – System limits.

  • rf (RfEvent) – Pulse that opens the repetition; a refocusing pulse gives the readout half of a spin-echo EPI.

  • gz (GradEvent, default=None) – Selection gradient played with rf.

  • gz_reph (GradEvent, default=None) – Rephaser of gz, left-aligned in the first block after the pulse.

  • fov (float or sequence of float) – Field of view per encoded axis, readout first (m).

  • matrix (int or sequence of int) – Matrix size per encoded axis, readout first.

  • order (array_like, default=None) – Integer offsets from the shot origin, (etl,) or (etl, 2), one row per line. When given, etl to extent are ignored; None generates the train with make_epi_shot_offsets().

  • etl (int, default=None) – Lines per repetition. None is what one shot of scheme needs to cross the phase-encode matrix.

  • scheme ({'linear', 'caipi', 'zigzag'}, default='linear') – Ordering to generate. 'linear' steps by segments * acceleration every line within one partition; 'caipi' adds the partition sawtooth of blipped-CAIPI; 'zigzag' traverses up and down a phase-encode segment instead of across the matrix.

  • acceleration (int, default=1) – Phase-encode undersampling Ry: the number of lines a blip steps.

  • segments (int, default=1) – Shots S the lattice is interleaved across. The blip becomes S * Ry, which shortens the train.

  • partition_acceleration (int, default=1) – Partition undersampling Rz, the height of the CAIPI cycle. 'caipi' only.

  • caipi_shift (int, default=1) – Partitions the pattern climbs per acquired line. 'caipi' only.

  • extent (int, default=None) – Phase-encode lines one pass spans. Required by 'zigzag' and rejected by the other schemes.

  • te (float, default=None) – Echo time (s), from the RF centre to the echo of line te_line. None is as short as possible. Consecutive echoes are esp apart; echo_times lists them.

  • te_line (float, default=0.0) – Line, counted from zero and possibly fractional, that te is timed to.

  • tr (float, default=None) – Repetition time (s) of the whole module, including the longest echo shift. None is as short as possible.

  • navigator_lines (int, default=0) – Lines read without blips between the read and phase-encode prewinders, for odd-even phase correction. Rejected with flyback.

  • echo_shifts (int, default=1) – Number of shots whose trains are delayed by successive multiples of esp / echo_shifts. The module plays no shift: the caller lengthens wait_shift.

  • oversampling (float, default=1.0) – Readout oversampling: the sample spacing in k-space is 1 / (oversampling * fov).

  • readout_bandwidth_hz (float, default=500000.0) – Requested ADC sampling rate (Hz); bandwidth_hz is the rate achieved on the ADC and gradient rasters.

  • ramp_sampling (bool, default=True) – Sample across the read ramps as well as the plateau, which shortens the echo spacing. False restricts the window to the plateau.

  • flyback (bool, default=False) – Read every line in the same direction, rewinding between lines, instead of alternating polarity. Adds a rewind per line and removes the odd-even echo inconsistency of a bipolar train.

  • spoiling_cycles (float, default=0.0) – Cycles of read-axis dephasing across voxel_size_m left at the end of the repetition.

  • voxel_size_m (float, default=None) – Length the spoiling is counted over (m); None is the readout resolution.

  • labels (sequence of str, default=None) – Names of the labels holding the shot origin of each encoded axis, phase encode first: two for a 3D train, one for 2D.

  • trigger (TriggerEvent or DigitalOutputEvent, default=None) – Event armed on the prewinder block.

Attributes:

  • rf (RfEvent) – The excitation.

  • gz (GradEvent) – Its selection gradient, if one was given.

  • gz_reph (GradEvent) – Its rephaser, if one was given, left-aligned in whichever block follows the pulse.

  • gx_pre (TrapEvent) – Read prewinder, half a line lobe against the first line’s polarity, right-aligned in the prewinder block so that it ends where the first line starts.

  • gx (list of GradEvent) – One read lobe per line: alternating polarity for a blipped train, the same lobe every time for a flyback one.

  • gx_navigator (list of GradEvent) – One read lobe per navigator line, the first positive; present only with navigator_lines.

  • gx_flyback (TrapEvent) – The rewind between two lines. flyback only.

  • gy_blips, gz_blips (list) – One phase-encode event per line, etl long, with None where no step is taken. In a blipped train an entry carries the second half of the step into the line and the first half of the step to the next line out of it; in a flyback train it is the whole step, played in the rewind block after that line, so the last entry is None. gz_blips is 3D only.

  • gy_pre, gz_pre (TrapEvent) – Phase encodes at their largest step, to be scaled per shot, right-aligned with gx_pre; with navigator_lines they play in a block of their own after the navigator. gz_pre is 3D only.

  • gy_rew, gz_rew (TrapEvent) – The negated encodes, left-aligned in the last block of the repetition.

  • gx_spoil (GradEvent) – Read-axis trapezoid in the last block: returns the read axis from the end of the last line to k = 0 and adds the spoiling.

  • adc (AdcEvent) – The acquisition window, shared by every line.

  • shot_labels (tuple of LabelSetEvent) – SET events on the prewinder block, one per name in labels, for the caller to set to the shot origin. Always a tuple, so it can be unpacked into a block.

  • line_labels (tuple of tuple of LabelIncEvent) – Per line, the INC events that step from the previous line to this one; empty for the first line, which shot_labels places.

  • order (numpy.ndarray) – Integer (etl, 2) (ky, kz) offsets from the shot origin.

  • wait_te (DelayEvent) – Present only when te exceeds the minimum.

  • wait_shift (DelayEvent) – Echo-shift delay before the first train line, one block raster long; present only with echo_shifts above 1. The caller lengthens it by s * echo_shift_step on shot s and shortens wait_tr by the same amount.

  • wait_tr (DelayEvent) – Present when tr exceeds the minimum, or when echo_shifts is above 1, in which case it holds the longest shift.

  • etl (int) – Lines per repetition.

  • esp (float) – Echo spacing (s).

  • echo_times (numpy.ndarray) – Echo time of each line (s) from the RF centre, without echo shift.

  • echo_time (float) – Echo time (s) of line te_line, the time te specifies.

  • echo_shift_step (float) – Echo shift between successive shots (s), esp / echo_shifts on the block raster; zero with one shift.

  • bandwidth_hz (float) – Achieved ADC sampling rate 1 / dwell (Hz).

  • n_samples (int) – ADC samples per line.

  • delta_kx (float) – K-space sample spacing along the readout (1/m).

  • blip_span (float) – Duration of one phase-encode step (s), taken from the read ramps.

Raises:

ValueError – If a count is out of range, order is the wrong shape or steps outside the matrix, labels does not name one counter per encoded axis, te_line lies outside the train, a navigator is asked of a flyback train, or the requested TE or TR is shorter than the train can achieve.

Examples

>>> import pypulseqpp.sequences as design
>>> import pypulseqpp as pp
>>> system = pp.Opts(max_grad=50, grad_unit="mT/m", max_slew=180, slew_unit="T/m/s")
>>> slab = design.SpatialSelectiveExcitation(system, 15.0, 0.12, is_slab=True)
>>> epi = design.EpiReadout3D(
...     system, slab.rf, slab.gz, fov=(0.22, 0.22, 0.12), matrix=(64, 64, 32),
...     scheme="caipi", acceleration=2, segments=2, partition_acceleration=3,
...     labels=("LIN", "PAR"),
... )
>>> epi.etl
16

>>> sorted(set(int(step) for step in epi.order[1:, 1] - epi.order[:-1, 1]))
[-1, 2]
