Sampling and echo-ordering decisions for Cartesian acquisitions, where the
readout lies on a regular grid and the design freedom is in how the remaining
axes are covered.

Echo-planar imaging covers the phase-encoded axis within one or a few
excitations, which makes the time the train takes — and therefore the
geometric distortion off-resonance produces — a consequence of the shot count
and the undersampling factors. Fast spin echo covers it over a refocused
train, which turns the decay of that train into a k-space weighting and its
transform into the point-spread function of the acquisition.
