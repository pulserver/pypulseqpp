# Complete sequences

Configurations of the sequences the package ships, listed in
{doc}`../../../sequences`.

Each page varies the parameters that separate one application of a family from
another — the spoiling and contrast of the gradient-echo family, the
segmentation and acceleration that separate a structural echo-planar scan from
a functional time series, the echo-train ordering and refocusing-angle
modulation of a three-dimensional fast spin echo, and the interleaves of the
non-Cartesian trajectories — and shows the resulting timing diagram, sampling
pattern and scan duration.
