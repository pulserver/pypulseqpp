Gradient design#

Gradient events on one logical gradient axis, and the operations on them. Amplitudes are in Hz/m, slew rates in Hz/m/s, gradient areas in 1/m and times in s, except where a docstring states otherwise; Events and blocks describes the trapezoid and arbitrary gradient events.
